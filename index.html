About Me

I am a Computer Science graduate and aspiring penetration tester with a strong foundation in programming, networking, Linux, Windows, and web technologies.

My main focus is cybersecurity, particularly penetration testing and vulnerability assessment. I enjoy approaching systems from an attacker’s perspective to understand how vulnerabilities arise, how they can be validated safely, and how they can ultimately be mitigated.

Methodology
